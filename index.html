About the press

Made by someone who needed one.

Fifty Two Press started on a construction site in Queensland. The planners on the shelf were corporate and joyless, or pretty and useless. None of them asked the only question that mattered: how are you actually travelling? So we made one that does.

Why do I order on Amazon?

Every diary is printed by Amazon. Their print-on-demand is the only way we can offer hardcover and full-colour pages at this price, printed fresh for each order and shipped from the Amazon facility closest to you. The button takes you to Amazon to check out, with Amazon delivery and returns. We earn a small commission on top of our royalty, at no cost to you.

Which tier is mine?

The book inside is the same in all four. Pick by the job. Executive: you carry the whole operation. Manager: you run a department or project. Team Leader: your first step into leading people. Professional: serious work, no team to hide behind.

Is it dated?

No. All 52 weeks are undated, so you can start any Monday and skip nothing if you take a week off.

Do I need to be good at colouring?

No. The panels are simple line work. Ten minutes with any pen is the point, not the result.

Where does it ship?

Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Europe, through the Amazon store for each region.
